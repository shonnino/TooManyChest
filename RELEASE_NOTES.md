## TooManyChest 1.5.1

- Counts chests from local Fortnite replays with the original Chest Counter 1.5.1 analyzer.
- Refreshes counts for available replays without duplicating matches and saves a backup of the existing history.
- Includes the TooManyChest dashboard, in-game overlay and Pirate Shop token odds.

Download `toomanychests.exe` for Windows 10 or 11 and turn on replay recording in Fortnite.

Based on [LED-N's Fortnite Chest Counter 1.5.1](https://github.com/LED-N/Fortnite-ChestCounter/releases/tag/v1.5.1).
